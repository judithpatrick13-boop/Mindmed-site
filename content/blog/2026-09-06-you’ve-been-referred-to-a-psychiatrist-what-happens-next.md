Hearing "We think a psychiatrist should see you too" can feel scary.




Maybe your MindMed psychologist said it during therapy.

Right away the thoughts come: Does this mean I’m really bad? Will I be forced to take drugs? Will I be admitted?




Take a breath. A referral is not a bad thing. It’s us making sure you get complete care.




Here’s exactly what happens after your psychologist refers you to a psychiatrist in Nigeria.




## **What’s The Difference? Psychologist vs Psychiatrist**




**MindMed Psychologist:** We talk with you. We help with thoughts, emotions, trauma, relationships, coping skills. We do therapy. We cannot prescribe medication.




**Psychiatrist:** A medical doctor who specializes in mental health. They can diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medication, and manage the biological side of mental health.




Think of it like this:

Therapy & Psychiatry = Full care for your mind and body




Many people see both at the same time. They work together.




## **Why Would Your Psychologist Refer You?**




We only refer when we think it will help you get better, faster. Common reasons:




1. **Medication could help:** For moderate-severe depression, anxiety, ADHD, bipolar, psychosis, severe insomnia

2. **Need for medical diagnosis:** To rule out thyroid issues, vitamin deficiencies, or other health causes

3. **Therapy alone isn’t enough right now:** You’re stuck, and your brain chemistry needs support too

4. **Safety check:** If there are risks we want a medical doctor involved




It doesn’t mean therapy "failed". It means we care enough to get you more support.




## **What Happens In Your First Psychiatrist Appointment**




Most psychiatric consults in Nigeria are now online. Here’s what to expect:




#### **1. Check-in & Confidentiality**

The psychiatrist will explain that what you say is private.




#### **2. Assessment Conversation**

They’ll ask about:

\- What you’ve been going through and for how long

\- Sleep, appetite, energy, concentration

\- Medical history and other medications, including herbal remedies

\- Family mental health history

\- Safety: "Have you had thoughts of hurting yourself?"




#### **3. Feedback & Treatment Plan**

They may share a diagnosis or "working impression". Then discuss options:

\- **Medication:** What it does, side effects, timeline. Most take 2-6 weeks

\- **No medication yet:** Maybe more tests or therapy first

\- **Blood tests:** To check for medical causes

\- **Follow-up:** Usually in 2-4 weeks




#### **4. Collaboration With Your Psychologist**

With your permission, your psychiatrist and MindMed psychologist will coordinate. So your medication and therapy support each other.




## **5 Myths About Seeing A Psychiatrist in Nigeria**




#### **Myth 1: "They will lock me up"**

Reality: Almost all care is outpatient. Hospitalization only happens if there’s immediate danger.




#### **Myth 2: "They’ll force medication on me"**

Reality: It’s a conversation. You decide together.




#### **Myth 3: "Medication means I’m weak or 'mad’”**

Reality: It’s medical care. Like insulin for diabetes. It can help your brain so therapy works better.




#### **Myth 4: "I’ll be on medication forever"**

Reality: Some people use it short-term. Some longer. It depends on you.




#### **Myth 5: "It’s too expensive"**

Reality: Online psychiatric consults in Nigeria start around ₦15,000. Some HMOs cover it.

## **How To Prepare For The Appointment**

#### 
Before The Appointment:

1. **Journal your symptoms:** When did they start? What makes them worse/better?

2. **List everything you take:** Prescription, supplements, herbal teas

3. **Write questions:** "What are the side effects?" "How will I know it’s working?"

4. **Set up privacy:** Quiet room, headphones, good data for online sessions

5. **Bring someone:** If that makes you feel safer




#### **After The Appointment**




1. **Fill your prescription** at a pharmacy if given one

2. **Track changes:** Mood, sleep, side effects. Tell both your psychiatrist and psychologist

3. **Keep coming to therapy:** Meds can stabilize. Therapy helps you heal.

4. **Be patient:** It takes time. Don’t stop anything without talking to your doctor first.
