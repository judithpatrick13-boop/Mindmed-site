## Why Nigerian Companies Are Adding Mental Health Support in 2026

This isn’t about being “woke.”

**1. Retention.** People don’t just leave for money. They leave where they feel unseen. When you say “we support your mental health,” you’re saying “we care about you as a person, not just output.” That sticks.

**2. Productivity.** An anxious mind can’t focus. A depressed mind makes mistakes. A burnt-out mind quits. Support helps staff process stress before it affects their work. That’s cheaper than fixing errors or hiring again.

**3. Recruitment.** More candidates are asking upfront: “What mental health support do you offer?” If the honest answer is “we have tea and biscuits,” you’re losing talent to companies with a real answer.
