## **What The Research Actually Says**

Online therapy isn’t experimental anymore.




Multiple studies from the American Psychological Association and WHO show that video therapy for anxiety, depression, stress, and relationship issues is just as effective as in-person therapy.




Why? Because therapy works because of 3 things:

1. A therapist who knows what they’re doing

2. A safe, confidential space

3. Tools and skills you practice between sessions




The screen doesn’t change any of that. The relationship does.




If you feel heard, safe, and you’re learning skills, it works. Whether you’re on a couch in Epe or on your bed in Enugu.
