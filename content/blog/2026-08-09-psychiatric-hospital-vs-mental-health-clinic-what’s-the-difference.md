If you or someone you love needs help, you’ve probably searched “psychiatric hospital near me” or “mental health clinic near me.”

And you’re confused. Aren’t they the same thing?

Short answer: no.

In Nigeria, we tend to use both terms to mean “anywhere for mental health.” But they’re different in three big ways: purpose, practitioners, and treatment. Choosing wrong can waste money, waste time, or make you more scared to get help. Let’s fix that.

## What Is A Psychiatric Hospital?

Think: “hospital for severe mental health conditions.”

What happens there:

**1.    Inpatient care.** You can be admitted for days or weeks. Examples include Yaba Psychiatric Hospital, Neuropsychiatric Hospital Aro, and Federal Neuropsychiatric Hospital Benin.

**2.	Severe cases.** Schizophrenia, bipolar disorder in crisis, severe depression with suicide risk, drug-induced psychosis, severe addiction.

**3.	Emergency care.** 24/7 support for people in crisis.

The vibe: clinical, medical, like a general hospital but for the brain.

The challenge: because of the word “hospital,” many Nigerians are scared. “Na mad people place.” That stigma makes people wait until it’s too late.

## **What Is A Mental Health Clinic Or Therapy Service?**

Think: “a doctor’s office for your mind.”

What happens there:

**1.	Outpatient only**. You come for a session and go home. No admission.

**2.	Everyday issues.** Anxiety, depression, stress, JAPA pressure, burnout, trauma, relationship problems, marriage pressure, grief.

**3.	Talk therapy plus skills.** The focus is talking, learning coping tools, and healing.

**4.	Medication if needed.** A psychiatrist will be consulted, but most of the care is therapy itself.

The vibe: private, calm, conversational, like seeing your GP for a headache, but for your emotions.

Who goes here: students, professionals, parents, couples, people who are functioning but struggling.

## The Biggest Difference: Who You Will See

This is where most people get lost. The title matters.

**Psychiatrist, the medical doctor.** Found at psychiatric hospitals and some clinics. Went to medical school and specialized in psychiatry. Can prescribe medication. Focus: diagnosing mental illness and managing medication for conditions like bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and severe depression. Sessions are usually short, 15 to 20 minutes, focused on symptoms and medication.

**Psychologist, the assessment expert.** Found at clinics, hospitals, and private practice. Holds a master’s or PhD in psychology. Cannot prescribe medication in Nigeria. Focus: testing, assessment, and therapy, diagnosing but treating through therapy rather than medication. Conducts IQ tests, trauma assessments, and personality tests.

**Therapist or counselor,** the talk therapy expert. Found at clinics and therapy platforms like MindMed. Trained in counseling, social work, or psychology. Cannot prescribe medication. Focus: full-length talk sessions covering anxiety, relationships, grief, self-esteem, and coping skills. This is who you’d typically talk to weekly.

Simple way to remember: psychiatrist means pills, psychologist means testing plus therapy, therapist or counselor means talking plus skills.

At a psychiatric hospital, you’ll mainly see psychiatrists and nurses. At a mental health clinic or therapy service, you’ll mainly see psychologists and therapists, with a psychiatrist consulted only if medication is needed.

## So What’s The Real Difference?

Psychiatric hospital means crisis, medication, and admission. Mental health clinic or therapy service means therapy, support, and outpatient care.

#### Go to a psychiatric hospital if:

•	You feel you might hurt yourself or someone else

•	You can’t eat, sleep, or function for days

•	You’re hearing voices or having severe psychosis

•	You need 24/7 monitoring

#### Go to a mental health clinic or therapy service if:

•	You’re anxious but still going to work

•	You’re sad, overwhelmed, or burnt out

•	You’re having relationship or family problems

•	You want to talk to someone before things get worse

Rule of thumb: hospital is for emergencies, therapy is for maintenance, healing, and prevention.

## FAQ

**Can a therapist prescribe medication?**

No. Only a psychiatrist can prescribe. Therapists focus on talk therapy, and will refer you if medication seems necessary.

**Do I need to go to a hospital first before therapy?**

No. You can start with therapy. If your therapist notices you need medical care, they’ll refer you at that point.

**Is therapy for “serious” problems only?**

No. People use therapy for stress, decision-making, and personal growth too. You don’t have to be in crisis to benefit.
