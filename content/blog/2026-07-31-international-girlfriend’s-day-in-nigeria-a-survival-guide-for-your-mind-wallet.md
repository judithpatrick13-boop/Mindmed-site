Tomorrow is International Girlfriend’s Day.




In other countries, it’s "celebrate all the girls."

In Nigeria? It’s "post your girlfriend, buy her gifts, or explain yourself on Twitter."




Suddenly IG and TikTok are full of flowers, iPhone 17s, spa days, hotel bookings, and "soft life" captions.

And if you’re single? Or your own person hasn’t said anything? Or money is tight?




That tight feeling in your chest has a name. It’s anxiety.

So let’s talk about it. No judgment. Just truth, peace, and boundaries.




## **To The Single Pringles: This Is Still Your Day**

I know. Seeing everyone post "My person ❤️" can feel like a punch to the gut.




Your brain starts: "What’s wrong with me? Why is it not me? Am I running out of time?"




But here’s what we forget: **You don’t need a relationship to be celebrated.**




International Girlfriend’s Day can literally mean girlfriends too. Your girls. The ones who checked on you during your breakdown at 2am. The ones who sent you ₦2k for transport. The ones who hyped you when you got the job.




Celebrate each other.

Buy yourself flowers. Take yourself out. Post yourself. Cook your favorite food. Your girl group chat is where you get the most support anyway.




Loneliness and \[depression thrive in comparison](/is-therapy-worth-therapy). Don’t give it that power.




**MindMed reminder**: If today makes you feel "less than," that’s a thought to question, not a fact. You are whole, with or without a title. Your worth isn’t determined by relationship status.




And if the sadness feels heavy for more than a few days, please talk to someone. You don’t have to carry it alone.




## **To The Guys: Don’t Go Broke for the Gram**

Bro. Breathe.




We see the pressure. "If you don’t post me, you don’t love me." "Where’s my gift?" "Even Tunde posted his babe."




Bro, that ₦200k you want to borrow for flowers? September you will hate  August you.




Love is not measured by how much you spend in 24 hours.

A ₦200,000 gift you borrowed money for will turn into September anxiety. And that money anxiety will enter the relationship as arguments, silence, and stress.




Do what’s within your pocket.

Text her. Call her. Cook for her. Write her a note. Plan a walk. Make a playlist. Give her your time.

Consistency is better than grand gestures. Every time.




**Boundary**: If she pressures you to spend beyond what you have, that’s a conversation, not a fight. Try: "I love you and I want to celebrate you, but I also need to protect our future and my peace. Can we do something that feels good for both of us?"

Setting boundaries in dating is how healthy relationships are built. Real partners understand that.




## **To The Ladies In Relationships: Don’t Test Him Today**

Sis, I know the timeline is tempting you.




But pressuring your person for gifts because "everyone is posting" is how [money anxiety ](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/money-anxiety-nigeria/)and resentment start.




Ask yourself honestly: Do I want the gift, or do I want to not feel left out?

There’s a difference. One is about love. The other is about fear.




If he shows up with time, words, effort, and presence, that’s love too.

If he truly can’t afford it right now, grace goes a long way. The relationship will last longer than August 1st. Debt won’t.




**Boundary**: You can say "I’d love to celebrate us today. What can we do together?" without saying "If you don’t buy me this, it’s over."

Healthy relationships are built on communication, not competition. Protect the relationship and protect his peace too.




## **The Real Link: Money, Anxiety, and Boundaries**

Here’s why we’re talking about this at MindMed:




#### **1. Money Anxiety**

Trying to "keep up" on social media is a fast track to debt and depression. Borrow money for gifts today, spend September dodging alerts and feeling ashamed. That’s not love. That’s pressure.

Read more on how to handle it here: [Money Anxiety in Nigeria](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/money-anxiety-nigeria/): How to Stop Stressing About Money




#### **2. Comparison and Anxiety**

Scrolling for 2 hours and feeling sad is valid. Your nervous system is reacting to images that aren’t your reality.

When anxiety spikes, log off. Go outside. Call a friend. Your worth isn’t in a post, a gift, or a status.

If worry and panic feel constant, you can learn tools to manage it: What Is Anxiety? [Panic Attacks Vs Anxiety ](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/Panic-attack-vs-anxiety-Nigeria/)

#### **3. Boundaries**

In dating, in friendship, with yourself.

"I will celebrate within my means."

"I will not let one day define my relationship."

"I will celebrate my single friends too."

"I will ask for what I need without threats."

Boundaries are love. For you and for them. Learn how: [How to Set Boundaries Without Guilt](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/how-to-set-boundaries-with-family-nigeria/)




That’s mental health work. That’s the work that keeps you sane in August and in December.




## **3 Ways To Celebrate Tomorrow Without Losing Your Mind**




#### **If You’re Single:**

Girl’s Day Brunch. Spa at home with music. Buy yourself that thing you’ve been eyeing. Text 3 friends "I appreciate you." Take solo pictures. You are the girlfriend. You are the prize.




#### **If You’re Taken:**

Celebrate each other, not the internet. A handwritten letter. A playlist for each other. Cooking together. 20 minutes of no-phone time just talking. A walk. Intention beats price tags.




#### **If You’re The Guy:**

Be intentional. Ask her: "What would make you feel loved today that won’t stress us?" Then do that. If it’s words, give words. If it’s time, give time. If it’s a gift and you can, give it. But don’t lie to yourself or to her.




## **What If Today Triggers You?**

It’s okay if today feels hard.




Maybe you just got out of a relationship.

Maybe you’re hoping someone will text you.

Maybe money is really tight and you feel ashamed.




All of that is human.

The goal isn’t to pretend you’re fine. The goal is to not let one day decide how you feel about yourself for the next 3 months.




Talk to a friend. Journal. Pray. Move your body. And if it feels too heavy, reach out for support.




## **Love Isn’t A Deadline**

International Girlfriend’s Day is one day.




Your mental health, your money, and your relationships are for 365 days.




So single girls, you’re winning. Celebrate your girls.

Taken girls, love gently and communicate clearly.

Guys, love within your means and with integrity.




And everyone: celebrate the women around you. Friend or girlfriend.
