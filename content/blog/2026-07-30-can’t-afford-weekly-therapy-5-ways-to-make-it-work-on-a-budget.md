But here’s what nobody tells you: **You don’t need weekly therapy to start healing**.

And you don’t need to be rich to afford it.




At MindMed, we believe cost shouldn’t be the reason you suffer in silence. That’s why we built different ways to get support, from **₦4,000 group sessions** to **₦7,000 individual sessions**, session packages, and self-help toolkits you can use at home.

\- Traffic: 3 hours

\- Taking leave from work

\- "What if my neighbor sees me enter a therapist’s office?"




**Online therapy fixes that**. And it’s often cheaper.

## **What NOT To Do**

1. **Don’t wait until you "have more money"** - Start with 1 session at ₦7,000 or grab a toolkit. That’s a decision, not a luxury.

2. **Don’t do 1 session and disappear** - Healing takes consistency. Try the 5-session package or bi-weekly instead. [Session 1 is just an assessment](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/is-one-therapy-session-enough/).

3. **Don’t compare** - Your friend paying ₦80k doesn’t mean you have to. Find what works for you.
