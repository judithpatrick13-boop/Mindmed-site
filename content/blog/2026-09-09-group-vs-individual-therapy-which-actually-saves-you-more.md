## **First: What’s The Difference?**




**Individual Therapy** is just you and your psychologist for 50 minutes.

It’s private. It’s all about you. We go as deep as you want, at your pace.




**Group Therapy** is you and 4 to 10 other people and 1 or 2 MindMed psychologists.

Sessions run for 90 minutes. Everyone is working on similar things; anxiety, stress, relationships, etc.

You learn from the therapist AND from each other.




Both are online. Both are led by licensed psychologists.

## **When Individual Therapy Makes More Sense**




Pick 1-on-1 if you need:




#### **1. Total privacy**

For trauma, marriage issues, addiction, or anything you’re not ready to share in a group yet.




#### **2. Laser-focused attention**

The whole 50 minutes is about you. Your goals, your story, your homework.




#### **3. Faster results on one specific problem**

Like interview anxiety, breakup recovery, or panic attacks. We can move quickly.




**Best deal for individual**: The ₦32,500 package for 5 sessions.

Therapy works best when it’s consistent. This package helps you stay consistent and saves you money.




If you did weekly sessions, 5 weeks would cost ₦32,500 instead of ₦35,000.




## **When Group Therapy Makes More Sense**




Pick group if you want:




#### **1. To save money**

At ₦4,000 per session, 4 weeks of group is ₦16,000.

4 weeks of individual is ₦28,000.

You’re saving ₦12,000 a month.




#### **2. To feel less alone**

There’s something powerful about hearing "I feel that too." In Nigeria we’re taught to hide struggles. Group breaks that silence.




#### **3. To learn from other people**

Someone in group might share a coping tool that changes your life. You get 10 perspectives, not just 1.




#### **4. To practice real-life skills**

Great for social anxiety, communication, loneliness. You practice in a safe room with real people.




And because it’s 90 minutes, you get more time to talk, listen, and practice.




## **The Honest Truth: Pros and Cons**




**Individual is best for** privacy, deep work, and moving fast.

The trade-off is it costs more.




**Group is best for** affordability, community, and learning from others.

The trade-off is you share the therapist’s time.




Neither is "better". They just do different things.




## **“But Will Group Actually Work?"**




3 common fears:




#### **“Do I have to talk?"**

No. You can listen for the first few sessions. A lot of healing happens just by listening.




#### **"What if I know someone there?"**

We group people carefully. We also have a strict rule: what’s said in group, stays in group.




#### **“Is it less effective?"**

Research says no. For anxiety, depression, and life skills, group therapy works just as well as individual. Sometimes better, because of the support.
