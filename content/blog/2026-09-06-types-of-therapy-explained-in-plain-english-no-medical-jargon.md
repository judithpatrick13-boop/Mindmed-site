So let’s break down the most common types of therapy in plain Nigerian English. No big grammar.




## **1. CBT: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy**

**Plain English:** "Let’s check your thoughts and change the ones making you suffer"




**What it is:** CBT is about the link between Thoughts → Feelings → Actions.

If your thought is "I will fail this interview", you feel anxious, then you avoid applying. CBT helps you catch that thought and test if it’s true.




#### **What happens in session:**

Your therapist asks: "What went through your mind?" "What’s the evidence for and against this thought?" Then you practice new ways to think.




**Best for:** Anxiety, depression, overthinking, procrastination, panic attacks




#### **Example:**

Thought: "If I don’t reply my boss in 2 mins, he’ll fire me" → Anxiety → You can’t sleep

CBT helps you challenge: "Is that true? Has he ever fired someone for that?"




## **2. Psychodynamic Therapy**

**Plain English:** "Let’s understand how your past is affecting your present"




**What it is:** This one digs into childhood, relationships, and patterns you don’t even notice. The idea: a lot of what we do now comes from way back.




#### **What happens in session:**

More open conversation. "Tell me about your parents" "Why do you think you keep dating the same type of person?"




**Best for:** Relationship issues, low self-esteem, repeating the same problems, "I don’t know why I feel like this"




#### **Example:**

You keep people-pleasing at work. In therapy you realize: "I had to keep my parents happy growing up so I wouldn’t get shouted at."




## **3. DBT: Dialectical Behavior Therapy:**

Plain English*: "How to handle big emotions without blowing up or shutting down"




**What it is:** DBT was made for people with very intense emotions. It teaches 4 skills: Mindfulness, Distress Tolerance, Emotion Regulation, and Relationship Skills.




#### **What happens in session:**

Very practical. You get worksheets and skills to use at the moment. "When I’m angry, do this instead of shouting."




**Best for:** Mood swings, self-harm thoughts, borderline traits, anger, feeling "too much"




#### **Example:**

Instead of blocking everyone and deleting WhatsApp when you’re upset, DBT teaches you: "Breathe, ride the wave, then respond."




## **4. ACT: Acceptance and Commitment Therapy**

**Plain English:** "Stop fighting your feelings. Do what matters anyway."




**What it is:** ACT says you can’t control every thought. Instead, accept the pain and still live by your values. "I’m anxious AND I’ll still go for the interview."




#### **What happens in session:**

Lots of exercises and metaphors. "Thoughts are like cars on the road. You don’t have to jump in every one."




**Best for:** Anxiety, chronic stress, grief, "I’m tired of trying to be positive all the time"




## **5. EMDR: Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing**

**Plain English:** "Therapy to help process trauma so it stops haunting you"




**What it is:** Used mainly for PTSD and trauma. While you recall a memory, the therapist guides you with eye movements or tapping. It helps your brain "file" the memory properly so it’s less painful.




#### **What happens in session:**

You focus on a bad memory while following the therapist’s fingers with your eyes. Sounds weird, but research backs it.




**Best for:** Trauma, flashbacks, nightmares, accident survivors, assault survivors




## **6. Solution-Focused Therapy**

**Plain English:** "Less gist about the problem. More action on the solution."




**What it is:** Short-term. Instead of spending 10 sessions on "why", we ask "what do you want to be different next week?"




#### **What happens in session:**

Goal-setting. "On a scale of 1-10, how close are you to your goal? What would make it 1 point higher?"




**Best for:** Specific problems, work stress, decision making, people who want quick results




## **7. Family/Relationship Therapy**

**Plain English**: "Let’s fix how we’re talking to each other"




**What it is:** Not just for married couples. Can be siblings, parent-child, or business partners. The "problem" is the relationship pattern, not one person.




**Best for:** Communication issues, conflict, marriage counseling




#### **So... Which One Is Right For Me?**




Here’s the honest truth: There’s no "best" therapy. There’s "best for you right now".




At MindMed, most of our psychologists use **CBT as the base** because it’s practical and research-backed. But we blend it.




Example:

Depression + low self-esteem = CBT + Psychodynamic

Anxiety + panic = CBT + ACT

Trauma = EMDR + CBT




It’s not one-size-fits-all.

## **3 Questions To Ask Your Therapist**




1. "What type of therapy do you use and why for me?"

2. "How will I know it’s working?"

3. "How long do you think this will take?"




If they can’t explain it in plain English, find someone else.
