Therapy is not Panadol. And your first session is not magic.




You finally did it. You booked therapy.




You sit down, take a deep breath, and tell a stranger the stuff you’ve been carrying alone.

You cry. You laugh. You feel 10kg lighter when you log off.




And somewhere in your head you think: "Okay. I’m good now. One and done."




Then two weeks pass.

The overthinking comes back. You and your partner have the same fight. The sadness creeps in again.




And the thought hits: "See? Therapy doesn’t work."




If this sounds like you, I need you to hear this: **You didn’t fail. And therapy didn’t fail.**

The expectation is just off.




In Nigeria, we treat therapy like paracetamol. Headache? Take 2 and wait 30 minutes.

But healing doesn’t work like that. Let’s talk about it.




## **What Actually Happens in Your FIRST Session**

First, let’s clear this up because nobody tells you:




**Session 1 is mostly assessment.**

It’s not where we "fix" you.




Here’s what really happens:

1. **We get to know each other**. Can you trust me? Can I understand you?

2. **We map out what’s going on**. When did this start? What makes it better? What makes it worse?

3. **We talk safety**. If you’re in crisis, we make a plan.

4. **We set goals**. What do you actually want from therapy?




That’s it. No worksheets. No deep trauma work. No "3 tools to fix your anxiety" yet.




Think of it like your first visit to a doctor. They ask questions, run tests, listen to your chest. They don’t do surgery that day.




So if you left session 1 feeling heard but not "cured" — that’s normal. That’s exactly what it’s supposed to be.




## **What One Session CAN Give You**

Even though it’s just assessment, session 1 is still powerful.




**In one session you can get:**

1. **Relief from saying it out loud**. Many Nigerians have never said "I’m depressed" to anyone. Saying it matters.

2. **Clarity**. "Oh... so this isn’t just me being lazy. This is burnout."

3. **Hope**. "Someone gets it. I’m not crazy."

4. **A plan.** "Okay, next time we’ll start working on X."




It’s like the first day at the gym. You’re sore, you’re tired, but you know where to start now.




## **What One Session CANNOT Do**

And this is where people get disappointed.




**One session cannot:**

1. **Undo 15, 20, 30 years in 50 minutes**

 That pattern of people-pleasing? That fear of [family conflict](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/how-to-set-boundaries-with-family-nigeria/). Tha[t anxiety and depression](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/What-is-depression-signs-nigerians-ignorewh/) you’ve had since uni? It took years to learn. It takes time to unlearn.




2. **Change your life instantly**

Insight is not the same as change. Knowing "I have boundary issues" is step 1. Practicing boundaries for 2 months is step 2-10.




3. **Fix your marriage, job, or finances by itself**

Therapy gives you tools and support. But you still have to go live your life between sessions. And that’s [where most of the work happens](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/what-therapist-does-between-sessions/).




4. **Replace medicine or other help**

 For some things, you need therapy and medication and community. Not therapy alone.




Expecting one session to fix everything is like planting seeds and checking the next day for mangoes.




## **So... How Long Does Therapy Really Take?**

I wish I could give you an exact number. I can’t. It depends on you.




But here’s a honest guide:




**4-6 Sessions:** Good for one specific thing. "I’m having panic attacks and I need tools." "I lost someone and I need support grieving."




**3-6 Months:** For deeper stuff. Relationship patterns, ongoing [anxiety and depression](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/signs-you-need-therapy-nigeria-2026/), self-esteem. This is where you actually start seeing things shift in real life.




**6-12+ Months**: For trauma, complex family stuff, big life overhauls. Slow and steady.




The pattern is this:

Session 1-2: We figure out what’s going on

Session 3-6: We start working and practicing

Session 7+: Things start sticking




Consistency beats intensity. 30 minutes every week is better than 3 hours once.




## **Why So Many People in Nigeria Stop After 1**

Let’s talk about it openly:




#### **1. "It’s money"**

Therapy is an investment. Therapy can be expensve per session. So we want instant ROI.




#### **2. "It got uncomfortable"**

Session 1 feels good. Session 3 is when we start poking at the sore spots. And that’s when people ghost.




#### **3. "People will say I’m mad"**

The stigma is real. "Just pray about it." "Be strong." So we quit to avoid questions.




#### **4. "I didn’t feel different"**

Because we expected a miracle. And healing is boring. It’s small wins over time.




## **How Do You Know If You Should Keep Going?**

Give it at least 3-4 sessions before you decide. Then ask:




1. Do I feel even 10% more understood than before?

2. Do I trust my therapist?

3. Am I willing to try what we discuss outside the room?

4. Is this a good fit? If not, it’s okay to [switch therapists](https://www.mindmed.com.ng/blog/can-you-switch-therapists-nigeria/).




If the answer to 2 of those is yes, keep going.




## **Give Yourself Grace**

One session can change your life. It’s the day you said "I deserve help."




But healing is a process. It’s messy. It’s slow. It’s worth it.




If you went once and stopped, you can come back. No shame.

If you’re scared it won’t work, try 6 sessions and then decide.




You didn’t get here in one day. And you won’t get out in one day.

But you will get out.
