Let’s be honest.




In Nigeria, therapy still feels like a "nice to have".




"When I have extra money"

"After I pay school fees"

"When my life is more settled"

"That one is for rich people"




I get it. With fuel price, rent, and food, mental health feels like the last thing on the list.




But here’s the hard question: **If your mind is not well, can anything else in your life be well?**




## **Why We Call Therapy a "Luxury"**




1. **Money:** Therapy costs money. So does food. But we don’t skip food.

2. **Stigma:** "Strong people don’t need therapy." "Just pray about it."

3. **No immediate result:** You don’t swallow therapy and your headache disappears in 10 mins like Panadol.




So we file it under: Treat yourself when you can.




## **But What If Therapy Is Actually Maintenance?**




Think about it like this:




**Body:** You go to the gym, buy drugs, see a doctor when sick. Because if your body fails, everything fails.

**Phone:** You buy data, charge it, repair screen. Because if your phone dies, work stops.

**Car:** You do service so it doesn’t break down on 3rd mainland.




**Mind: ???** We wait until it breaks down.




Anxiety, burnout, anger, overthinking — these are not "luxury problems". These are daily problems that affect:

\- **Work**: Procrastination, can’t focus, calling in sick

\- **Relationships**: Snapping at partner, withdrawing from friends, people-pleasing

\- **Money:** Impulse spending to cope, can’t negotiate salary because of low confidence

\- **Health**: Insomnia, body pain, high BP from chronic stress




Waiting until "rock bottom" to get therapy is like waiting until your engine seizes before servicing your car. More expensive later.

## **Who Actually Needs Therapy?**




Not just "people with problems".




You need therapy if:

1. **You’re functioning but exhausted:** "I’m okay but I’m tired of being okay"

2. **You keep repeating patterns:** Same toxic relationships, same job burnout

3. **Life transition:** New job, loss, marriage, relocation, new baby

4. **You want to grow:** Better communication, confidence, emotional regulation

5. **You have a diagnosis:** Depression, anxiety, ADHD, etc, and need tools




If you’re human and living in Nigeria in 2026, you probably qualify.




## **How To Make Therapy Not Feel Like a Luxury**




1. **Reframe it:** It’s not "self-care Sunday". It’s "mental maintenance".

2. **Budget for it:** ₦7,000/week. Skip 2 takeouts. Or 1 data bundle.

3**. Start online:** No transport, no 3-hour Lagos traffic. Do it from your room.

4. **Use HMO:** Some companies now cover therapy. Ask HR.

## **Luxury vs Necessity: The MindMed Take**




Luxury is spa, vacation, new iPhone. Nice, but optional.




Necessity is anything that keeps you functioning.




If you can’t sleep, can’t focus, can’t connect, can’t regulate emotions, you’re not functioning at 100%.




Therapy gives you the tools to function, to work, to love, and to live.




That’s not luxury. It's the bare minimum.
